  <div class="sec-head"><div class="sec-no">06</div><h2>How capabilities form: research-supported mechanisms</h2></div>
  <p class="lead">Learning methods are matched to the learning target. Retrieval and spacing support retention; transfer draws more heavily on understanding, comparison across multiple instances, strong models, varied practice and use in new contexts. The National Academies and related reviews synthesize the evidence for these mechanisms.<span class="cite">[9][10]</span></p>

    <div class="method"><div class="n">1</div><div><h3>Define performance first</h3><p>Identify expert work, evaluation criteria and representative tasks before selecting a course. Capability goals must map to observable performance.</p></div></div>

    <div class="method"><div class="n">4</div><div><h3>Variation and comparison</h3><p>Practice across examples, problem types and contexts; compare similar and different cases to build more transferable concept boundaries.<span class="cite">[9]</span></p></div></div>
