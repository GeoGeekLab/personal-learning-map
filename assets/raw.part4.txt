  <div class="sec-head"><div class="sec-no">04</div><h2>Ten capability domains and five levels of depth</h2></div>
  <p class="lead">The ten domains define long-run capability; the five-level model manages depth. The model synthesizes learning science, university training practice and frameworks such as UNESCO’s Understand–Apply–Create for cross-domain progress management.<span class="cite">[3]</span></p>
  <div class="stage-strip">
    <div class="stage"><div class="n">0</div><b>Orient</b><p>Understand scope, standard, typical problems and common misconceptions.</p></div>
    <div class="stage"><div class="n">1</div><b>Learn</b><p>Complete a strong foundation and establish concepts, facts and methods.</p></div>
    <div class="stage"><div class="n">2</div><b>Practice</b><p>Build stable performance through problems, drills, experiments, writing or simulation.</p></div>
    <div class="stage"><div class="n">3</div><b>Apply</b><p>Use the capability in real projects, work, relationships or life decisions.</p></div>
    <div class="stage"><div class="n">4</div><b>Create</b><p>Solve open problems and produce work, methods, teaching or team outcomes.</p></div>
