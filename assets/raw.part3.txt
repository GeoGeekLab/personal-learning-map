<b>学习路径：</b>数字/数据素养 → 编程/计算基础 → AI 基础 → eval → workflow / system design。<br><b>表现证据：</b>能评估模型与系统，并区分长期能力、可迁移方法与阶段性工具。</div></div>
    <div class="cap-row"><div class="cap-no">08</div><div><h3>人文、伦理、公民与文明理解</h3><div class="def">历史、经济、社会、制度、伦理、审美、科技与社会、环境与可持续性。</div></div><div class="path"><b>学习路径：</b>高质量通识入口 → 原典/经典 → 多模型比较 → 当代数据与公共问题。Stanford、Harvard、MIT 均保留显著的人文/社会/伦理训练。<span class="cite">[4–6]</span><br><b>表现证据：</b>能区分事实、解释模型、价值判断与制度约束。</div></div>
    <div class="cap-row"><div class="cap-no">09</div><div><h3>专业深度与价值创造</h3><div class="def">在一个领域建立足够深度，处理高难度问题并产生可验证价值。</div></div><div class="path"><b>学习路径：</b>专业基础 → 真实项目 → 专家反馈 → 复杂系统 → 旗舰作品/专业贡献。<br><b>表现证据：</b>能解决本领域真实问题；作品、团队或市场可以独立验证质量。AI × GeoAI 可作为候选交叉方向，但需要持续用项目需求和比较优势验证。</div></div>
    <div class="cap-row"><div class="cap-no">10</div><div><h3>人生治理与长期责任</h3><div class="def">财富、风险、法律、关系、家庭、照护、共同体与价值排序。</div></div><div class="path"><b>学习路径：</b>先建立基本制度与安全网，再由生命周期事件触发专项学习。<br><b>表现证据：</b>现金流与风险制度、保险与应急方案、关系规则、照护准备和重大决策记录。</div></div>

<section data-section="methods">
  <div class="sec-head"><div class="sec-no">06</div><h2>能力形成：研究支持的学习机制</h2></div>
  <p class="lead">学习方法按目标类型配置。记忆保持主要受检索练习与间隔安排支持；跨情境迁移更多依赖理解、多个实例比较、强模型、变化练习与新情境调用。National Academies 与相关综述为这些机制提供研究依据。<span class="cite">[9][10]</span></p>
  <div class="method-grid">
    <div class="method"><div class="n">1</div><div><h3>先定义表现标准</h3><p>学习前先识别专家作品、评价标准和典型任务。能力目标以可观察表现作为完成标准。</p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">2</div><div><h3>检索练习</h3><p>在不查看材料时回忆、回答问题或重建结构，再立即纠错。研究支持它优于单纯重复阅读用于长期保持。<span class="cite">[9][10]</span></p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">3</div><div><h3>间隔安排</h3><p>把重要知识分布到多次学习。具体间隔根据保留期限和任务性质设置。<span class="cite">[9][10]</span></p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">4</div><div><h3>变化练习与比较</h3><p>在多个实例、问题类型和情境中练习；通过比较相似与不同案例，形成更可迁移的概念边界。<span class="cite">[9]</span></p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">5</div><div><h3>自我解释与模型</h3><p>解释成立机制、适用条件以及与旧知识的连接。结构理解为跨情境迁移提供基础。<span class="cite">[9]</span></p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">6</div><div><h3>目标练习与反馈</h3><p>每次练习针对可观察弱点，并尽快获取教师、同伴、用户、测试或行为数据反馈。深度知识的发展需要持续练习与反馈。<span class="cite">[11]</span></p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">7</div><div><h3>真实应用与外部对象</h3><p>让核心学习主线接触真实数据、真实用户、真实团队与真实决策，以检验能力在新情境中的稳定表现。</p></div></div>
    <div class="method"><div class="n">8</div><div><h3>学习网络</h3><p>为高价值专业建立稳定的同伴、导师或评审关系。课程提供输入，专业反馈决定下一轮练习方向。</p></div></div>
  </div>
  <div class="note"><b>季度实施。</b>每季度推进少数高优先级能力进入下一深度；健康、语言等基础能力保持低剂量维护；探索性主题可停留在 Orient 或 Learn。季度评价以能力证据、现实应用与反馈质量为主。</div>
</section>

<section data-section="scope">
  <div class="sec-head"><div class="sec-no">01</div><h2>Scope and method</h2></div>
  <p class="lead">The evidence is organized by function: WEF reports employer skill demand; OECD and UNESCO provide education goals and competency frameworks; universities specify undergraduate curriculum structures; learning science explains how knowledge and skills are formed, retained and transferred.</p>
  <div class="claim-grid">
    <div class="claim"><div class="num">4</div><h3>Evidence types</h3><p>Labour market, future education, university curricula, and learning science. The four evidence types inform demand, goals, curriculum structure and learning mechanisms respectively.</p></div>
    <div class="claim"><div class="num">10</div><h3>Capability domains</h3><p>These define long-run outcomes and are developed through different courses, projects and life practices over time.</p></div>
    <div class="claim"><div class="num">14</div><h3>Existing modules</h3><p>These remain the content and life-domain index. Modules organize material; capability domains define performance.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="note"><b>Source roles.</b> WEF’s <i>Future of Jobs 2025</i> provides employer views on current and rising skills. The OECD Learning Compass provides an education framework centred on student agency, well-being, knowledge/skills/attitudes/values and transformative competencies. UNESCO’s four-dimension framework organizes AI competency education.<span class="cite">[1–3]</span></div>
  <div class="subhead">Cross-framework synthesis</div>
  <p class="lead">The recurrent structure across sources includes health and self-regulation foundations; learning agency; quantitative, evidential and scientific reasoning; creativity and design; communication and collaboration; digital and AI literacy; ethics, citizenship and social understanding; sustainability; and genuine depth in at least one professional domain. Personal finance, family, law and care sit in the life-governance layer and extend areas that university and workforce frameworks cover less directly.</p>
